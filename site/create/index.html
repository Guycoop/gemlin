Bring it to your desktop

  1. Click Copy.
  2. Paste it into Gemlin's chat window on your desktop and press Enter. Gemlin changes right away.

Gemlin not awake? Paste it into a terminal instead (it's a gemlin look command), then run gemlin start. Haven't installed Gemlin yet? It takes a few minutes. Change your mind later? Come back, tweak, and copy again.
